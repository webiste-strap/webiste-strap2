How to use sports tape

Application depends on the tape and purpose. For injury treatment, follow guidance from a qualified professional.

1

Clean & dry

Apply tape to clean, dry skin and avoid irritated areas.

2

Apply carefully

Use the technique appropriate for the tape and the area being supported.

3

Check your skin

Stop using the tape if you develop pain, numbness, swelling, or skin irritation.
